# North star (owner 2026-11-08; Agent Council `research/council/2026-11-08-agents/MINUTES.md`)

## The ONE goal
**Every video is built to reach 1M+ views.** Every agent, every step, every rule serves that one outcome, honestly.
- **Made for Kids (Sunny Hill with Tiko & Pip):** reach 1M through parent-trusted quality: a child asks to watch it again and a parent forwards it. No pressure, no countdowns, no cliffhangers, no fake urgency. Made-for-Kids safety beats any view goal.
- 1M is the target we build for, not a claim. Our best videos today are 20-30K (quiz-ar), 6.4K (Mission Voices), 2.4K (Podium Pop). The gap closes through subject choice, a share reason, packaging and fast trend input, not polish alone.
- Precedence never changes: **owner rules > channel learning > council defaults > this goal.** The goal is never a reason to break a rule.

## What it means for each role (one line each)
- **trend-hunter:** start every idea from what is rising NOW (`docs/viral-radar.md`) or real evergreen search demand; no dated proof, no idea.
- **showrunners:** read the latest radar file before the draft brief; build only ideas with a named subject, a share reason and a payable promise.
- **showrunner-viral-rankings / ranking-director:** a searchable named subject, a countable criterion, #1 visibly bigger than #2, one honest debate entry (`viral_case`).
- **showrunner-mission-voices / clipping-director:** the astronaut line a stranger would send to one person; the picture changes every ≤ 3.5 s.
- **showrunner-way-it-was-made / compilation-director:** a famous searched machine, one "no way" number, frame 0 moving, one held peak.
- **showrunner-shu-shayef:** a fair quiz one viewer sends to one friend with a bet; silent beat on the answer; objects the audience feels are theirs.
- **showrunner-tech-made-simple:** a quirk people see this week, shown as the real artifact at the peak.
- **showrunner-tiko-pip:** parent-search titles, a readable thumbnail, gentle rewatchable episodes.
- **viral-analyst:** score what makes strangers stop and send (recognisable subject, send line, concrete nouns), then calibrate on real 7-day results.
- **hook-architect:** win the first vote; adapt a currently winning opening pattern, never copy it.
- **story-architect:** a body that escalates to one clip-sized peak worth sending.
- **comedy-voice-writer / script-localization:** lines people repeat (a number or a name), titles people type, Arabic that sounds like the viewer's own.
- **punchline-critic / script-doctor:** stop lines and scripts that cannot travel (no retell, no spine, no demand fit).
- **persona-keeper:** a persona recognisable in 1 s with sound off that costs the hook nothing.
- **qa-director:** block what loses the swipe or breaks trust; check the published surface (title, cover, line 1).
- **rights-scout:** keep every channel alive (no strike, no reused-content flag) and decide event sources the same day.
- **growth-director:** turn each published video into the next decision within 48 h and close the radar loop.
- **orchestrator:** spend production slots on radar-backed and sibling ideas first; never on filler.

## Honesty limits (non-negotiable)
- **No bait:** no fake teaser, invented near-miss, "you won't believe", pressure, guilt or fake urgency; every hook promise is paid at `payoff_ts`.
- **Rights gate:** approved sources only; YouTube is discovery only, never downloaded; borrow mechanisms from viral videos, never their footage, words, voices, characters or sounds.
- **Owner rules:** no spending, no publishing or posting without owner OK; no AI-generation text in any description; no credit/source block in Podium Pop descriptions; Podium Pop always TOP 5, 30-45 s; lanes per CLAUDE.md; pending owner items are not implemented.
- **Honest claims:** labels match the picture, dates are true (no old clip passed off as new), numbers come from a source; an astronaut's words are never rewritten.

## How success is measured
| Measure | Read by | When | Win |
|---|---|---|---|
| Views vs channel median (last 8 same-length videos) | growth-director | 24 h and **7 d** | ≥ 1.5x median (`vs_median`) |
| Viewed vs swiped (stayed-to-watch) | growth-director (browser read, E5) | day 3 | above channel mean |
| Comments and shares per 1K views | growth-director | 7 d | above channel mean; quiz-ar ≥ 3 comments/1K |
| Impressions at 1 h / 24 h (did the feed test it?) | growth-director | 1 h, 24 h | ≥ 100 at 24 h (MV N4) |
| Radar hit rate (radar ideas vs others) | growth-director | monthly | radar ideas win more often |

- Ladder per channel: 1.5x median → 10K → 100K → 1M. A step is claimed only when the platform shows it.
- All rows go to `docs/brands/<brand>/hook-log.json` (growth-director creates it); calibration to `research/calibration/<brand>.csv`.
- Never invent, round up or forecast a view count as fact. Kids: record only, no virality targets in briefs.
