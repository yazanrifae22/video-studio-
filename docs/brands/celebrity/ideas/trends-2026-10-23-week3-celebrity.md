## Method and caveats
- **Demand:** Wikimedia per-article pageviews (user agent): September 2026 monthly views, plus daily views for Oct 25-Nov 12 2025. The 2025 window shows whether the date itself drives a spike (e.g. Stanley 16x on Nov 10, Röntgen 6.5x on Nov 8). Pageviews show interest, not YouTube demand. Correlation only.
- **Competitors:** YouTube public search results (no login), scraped 2026-10-23. View counts come from the results page, so `verified: true` on the count. Shorts show no upload date, so views/day is unknown (`null`).
- **Facts:** each idea has 2 to 4 sources in `sources` (URL and access date 2026-10-23), plus a "weaknesses" list of conflicts between sources that the writer must respect.
- **Overlap:** checked against all 28 celebrity video titles (e.g. Marie Curie Nov 7, Howard Carter Nov 4, Grete Waitz Oct 31 and Rocky are already made, so they were excluded). No politics or elections (US midterms Nov 3), nothing defamatory, no minors as subjects. Stories that touch a subject's childhood (Emma Stone, Radcliffe, Lorde) use only the adult's own public retelling.
