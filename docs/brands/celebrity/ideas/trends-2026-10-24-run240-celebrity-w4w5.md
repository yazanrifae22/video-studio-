- **Demand:** Wikimedia monthly pageviews (Sept 2026, user agents) for the closest article. This measures interest, not YouTube demand. Correlation only.
- **Competitors:** YouTube public search ("<topic> #shorts"), no login, 2026-10-24. View counts come from the results page (`verified: true` when shown). Shorts show no upload date, so views/day is unknown. Some searches returned unrelated results (Grey Cup, Oasis, Treasure Island, Hubble); there the gap score rests on the lack of on-angle results.
- **Novelty:** checked against all 54 celebrity videos and 61 earlier celebrity ideas in `data/studio.db`. No person or story repeats. Same-person 7-day check passes. Topic clusters are listed under open questions.
