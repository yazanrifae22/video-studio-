## 3. 1M strategy
- **Format:** animated typography + calm narration, 9:16 Short, one named star, one number that does not fit, one dated turn. Shorts only; no long videos, no lists, no "explained".
- **Length:** 38-48 s, hard max 55 (our top seven: 46-60 s; 34 s videos did well too when the name was big).
- **Title pattern:** star + number/oddity. Examples: "Messi Won 2 Golden Balls. 2026 Went to Rodri"; "Harry Kane Was Loaned Out 4 Times". Reused winners: age/record numbers (Tyson, Pele, Yamal).
- **Hook default:** frame 0 = giant gold number (counter/digit swap) on near-black, readable in 1 s on mute; first word is the name or number; no face, logo, silhouette.
- **Story structure:** new playbook row `turning-point micro-story` (spine "[Name] had [low number], but [dated turn], therefore [outcome number]"); beats in the showrunner file; peak = the number held with silent voice; mid-payoff 40-55%.
- **Honest talk trigger:** pick-a-side with a reason, spoken once at 60-70% ("luck or the loan years?"), tied to a real debate (Rodri vs Messi, Kane's late trophy). No YES/NO cards, no comment-to-win.
- **Cadence:** start 1 new video/day (posting time by audience: US 74% of views, IN/GB/PH/CA next [data]). Peg videos jump the queue (publish within 7 d of the peg); raise to 2/day only after 5 videos reach >= 1.5x median (median now 189).
- **Legal guardrails:** (1) Defamation: living people, documented public record only, 2 sources, "reported" for contested lines, no crime/health/family/money allegations, no disparagement. (2) Likeness: no photos, stills, recognisable silhouettes or voices; names as text only (nominative use), nothing implying endorsement. (3) Trademark: no FIFA/Ballon d'Or/Rocky/Grammy logos, trophy art, team crests, font-alikes; League/team names as plain text. (4) Hot topics: no deaths, tragedies, trials, health. (5) Channel About text currently says "narration is an AI-generated voice" (`channel-kit.md`): owner rule bans AI-generation text in descriptions; orchestrator to decide if the About needs the same treatment (policy side: disclosure rules for realistic synthetic content do not apply to a clearly synthetic narrator, [opinion], growth-director to confirm).
- **Legacy queue coexistence:** the 53 public + about 19 private/scheduled legacy videos (scheduled to ~2026-11-13) keep going; new videos take priority slots, legacy fills the rest and stops if it would put > 1 legacy video per new one. Recommendation to the orchestrator/growth-director: hold the remaining long and non-sports legacy items, release only legacy Shorts with a named star and a number. I do not edit the ledger or schedule.
