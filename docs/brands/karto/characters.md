# Karto and Skip

Pose sheet: `docs/brands/karto/pose-sheet.jpg` · hero stills: `karto.jpg`, `skip.jpg` · drawn in code: `packages/cutout/web/kit/art.js`.

## Karto (كرتو), the host
A storyteller whose head is a cardboard box. He tells one wild true story per video and his whole job is to keep Skip
(and you) watching.
- **Look:** box head wider than tall, seen slightly from the side (top and right faces show the box depth). Two open
  flaps on top, deliberately uneven: a long one leaning out to the left, a short one on the right. A strip of masking tape
  repairs the top-right corner. Big white paper sticker eyes with marker pupils, thick marker brows, a wide marker mouth.
  Small darker-kraft body, a vermilion paper scarf with a fringed tail, paper-strip arms fixed with brass fasteners,
  standing on a popsicle stick.
- **Reads at 120 px as:** a brown box with two big white eyes, uneven flaps and a red scarf.
- **Not:** a robot, a printed shipping box, a cube-headed toy. No logos or printing on the box except faint "this side up"
  arrows.
- **Poses:** neutral, talk, point (mirror for left), shocked, thinking, laughing, deadpan, whisper, facepalm, present, cheer.

## Skip (سكيب), the viewer
Small (about 60 % of Karto), a sticky note with a phone, always one thumb away from skipping. He is the audience on
screen: bored, then hooked, then the first to comment.
- **Look:** head = a real yellow sticky note, a slightly tilted square with a darker glue strip on top and one peeled
  corner (lighter underside). The face is drawn straight on the note in marker: big sticker eyes, small brows, marker
  mouth. Body = a second, smaller sticky note with a drawn pocket. Short paper arms with brass fasteners. A phone whose
  screen shows a "skip" double arrow (or comment lines while typing). On a stick; in Shorts he stands on a small crate.
- **Reads at 120 px as:** a yellow square note with eyes and a blue phone.
- **Poses:** neutral, talk, point (mirror for left), shocked, thinking, laughing, deadpan (annoyed), whisper, skip (thumb over
  the button), mindblown, typing.

## How they act
Static cutouts: no limbs move. They act by swapping pose plates (with a pop), hopping, tilting, turning, sliding and the
automatic talk bob. Karto stands left, Skip right (wide); both in a row under the board (tall).
