## Do not say
- "Gold crashed 30% in a day" or any one-day crash: the largest 2026 one-day fall seen is -2.7%.
- "All-time low": the price is about 30% below its January peak, not at a record low.
- "All-time high US$5,608": unsourced; the sourced figures are US$5,589-5,592.
- "Central banks are selling gold": they were net buyers in Q1-Q3 2026. Russia and Turkey were net sellers in Q2 only.
- "A 15% fall will trigger panic": Fitch says lenders can absorb a first 15% fall.
- "Goldman says US$5,400 for 2027": Kitco (17 Nov) says US$4,650.
- "Gold is down X% this week" without a live price: the 14-19 Nov price is unknown.
- "Gold is about 2% of stock mined per year": about 1.6%.
- "Biggest drop since 1980" for April 2013: sources disagree (1980 vs 1983).
- Any current Egypt or Saudi gold price: only stale figures found.
- "Gold recovered its 2011 high in year X": not found.
- Kitco's "62% bearish": the page's own counts give about 77%.
- BeInCrypto's "bounce to US$4,136": date unclear, and it conflicts with the 14-month low headline.

- The honest word is "slide" or "correction" for the day-to-day, and "bear market" only for the 30%-from-peak view. There was no one-day crash in the sources I opened (largest -2.7%).
