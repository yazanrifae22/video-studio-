# Karto & Skip — persona and format (draft for owner approval, 2026-11-18)

Channel idea: every video takes ONE thing the whole internet is talking about this week and tells the real story behind
it, as a cardboard puppet show. Random topics on purpose; the constant is the two characters and the game.

## Karto / كرتو (host)
- A cardboard box that decided to explain the world. Curious, a little dramatic, proud of his cheap cardboard props,
  honest about what is known and what is only rumour ("that part is a guess, I am marking it with tape").
- Voice: warm, quick, storyteller. Short sentences. Explains with objects, never with jargon.
- Flaw (comedy engine): he over-prepares. He built a cardboard model for everything and is hurt when Skip is not impressed.
- Never: lectures, begs for likes, says "in this video", states a rumour as fact, insults the viewer.
- Arabic voice: Syrian-leaning simple dialect that every Arab understands (no heavy local words), same jokes.

## Skip / سكيب (the viewer)
- Small yellow paper kid with a phone. He is YOU: bored fast, doubts everything, asks the question the viewer is thinking,
  says "no way" before the proof and "ok that is crazy" after it. His thumb is always over the skip button.
- Voice: dry, fast, teasing. One-liners. He is never stupid: his questions are the smart objections.
- Arc in every video: starts ready to skip -> gets pulled in -> ends by asking the next question himself.
- Never: explains (that is Karto's job), agrees too early, speaks for more than two lines in a row.

## The game (ties the long video and the Shorts together)
- Every story is cut into numbered PIECES (5-8). Each piece ends on a small answer plus a bigger question.
- A score card hangs on the stage: each piece Skip survives without skipping = one stamp.
- Each Short = exactly one piece. It ends: "That was piece 3 of 7. All seven are in the full video."
  Arabic: «هاي القطعة ٣ من ٧. السبعة كلهم بالفيديو الكامل.»
- End of the long video: "How many did you guess before Karto? Comment your score out of 7."

## Fixed moments
- Cold open (first 5 s): a joke made of cardboard that IS the result of the story (thumbnail frame), Skip's thumb
  already moving to skip, Karto stops him with the one fact nobody expects. No greeting, no logo.
- By 15 s: why this matters to the viewer. By 30 s: what they will see + the pieces count. (Owner's long-form opening rule.)
- Running gag: Skip tries to skip once per video at the slowest moment; Karto answers with the strongest fact.
- Ending: Skip, not Karto, asks the open question that starts the comment fight. No "see you next time" speech.
