APV. Highest: Last Space Shuttle (81.7% across 55 seconds) and Perseverance (71.5%). Lowest: RS-25 (48.5%) and Hubble (48.9%).
Winners vs losers: (1) Immediate visual hook: concrete scale and visceral action at second zero. RS-25 shows an engine test firing; the Crawler opens with "6 million plus pounds...". Takeaways: Front-load the visual spectacle: "If a Short is about Rover stress-testing, open directly on the rover vibrating violently or inside the cryogenic chamber within the first 1.5 seconds rather than building up slowly." ### Q2. What is stopping my Shorts from reaching 1 million views? Be specific about hook, length, topic, title, and posting.
Reaching 1 million views on a Short requires mass appeal and high viewer retention. Your Shorts sit between 300 and 1,500 views, Viewed vs Swiped Away 41-68%, APV 48-82%. Top Shorts sustain 80%+ viewed and 110-130%+ APV (re-watches and seamless loops).
1. Hook (s0-2). Problem: openings rely on archival dates, locations, technical stage-setting ("July 8th, 2011...", "500 seconds on a Stennis test stand..."). A viewer decides in under 1.5 s. 40-60% swipe-away means over half drop before the premise. Fix: open with climax/conflict/absurdity. Instead of "500 seconds on a Stennis test stand..." use "NASA strapped a $100M rocket engine to the ground and burned it until the air turned white."
2. Length & loop. Most Shorts 30-55 s. Longer ones get decent completion (Last Space Shuttle 81.7%) but rarely loop. Fix: tight 18-25 s cuts with an unexpected ending or hidden visual detail; seamless loop: last line finishes the opening sentence.
3. Topic. SLS Core Stage in 5 Pieces and Mobile Launcher towers appeal to engineering enthusiasts only. Fix: human stakes, extreme danger, mind-bending scale: insane budgets, near-disasters, bizarre historical experiments, impossible feats (secret underground bases, extreme crash tests, machines so large they warp the ground).
4. Titles. They read like PBS chapter headings ("How Hubble Was Built, Broken and Fixed in Orbit", "The Seabees Built 111 Major Airstrips in WWII"). Fix: Hubble: "NASA Launched an $11B Telescope That Couldn't See"; Seabees: "How the U.S. Built Entire Runways in Under 72 Hours".
5. Posting & volume. Steady cadence needed; early catalog means slow pattern discovery. 3-5 Shorts a week; iterate immediately on what hits. Cut archival pauses; kinetic captions; whooshes, metallic impacts, sub-bass drops; cut every 1.5-2.5 s.
