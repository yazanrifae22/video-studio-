## PACING (owner 2026-11-02, overrides older length rules)
- Runtime **30–45 s** for every TOP 5 (16–20 s is too short). Narration slower: ~2.2–2.5 words/s (slower TTS pace), never rushed.
- "Slower" means 2.2–2.5 words/s with SHORT beats — NOT long gaps. Below ~2.0 words/s (QA saw 1.2–1.6) reads as dead air and fails retention. Fill length with more picture beats, keep speech flowing.
- After each rank reveal a 0.6–1.0 s silent beat (picture keeps moving, music only) so viewers can react/guess.
- Each entry = build-up + key moment + slow-mo or replay of the key beat. Get length from more beats, not long static shots (each shot ≤3.5 s).
- Like-ask at 50–60 %; end with a comment question on screen.

## 2. Format rules (F)
- **F1** TOP 5 ALWAYS (owner rule 2026-10-31: every ranking video is TOP 5, never TOP 3 or other counts; cutlist refuses non-top5). Five distinct moments from five distinct sources (max 2 per source, 30-day reuse cooldown; code-enforced). Rank = opinion (`ranking_criterion` says "my ranking"); never "best ever" as fact.
- **F2** Title is exactly `TOP 5 <WORD1> <WORD2> MOMENTS`. WORD1/WORD2 are topic nouns (hyphenated compound = one word), accent colours of the category preset (default yellow/red, nature mint/orange, skill cyan/magenta); TOP 5 and MOMENTS white. No hype words (funny, funniest, hottest, craziest, best, top, most, insane, epic, wild). Validator-enforced; `script.json` title must equal the burned title.
- **F3** 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264/AAC. Text inside the union safe box x 64-936, y 270-1420; credit line y ~1376-1400; no UI-zone text.
- **F4** Rank rail = the bigger default (TOP 5: inactive 130x100, active 170x128, `#N` 66/90 px). Active card carries a sublabel <= 12 chars from the entry's first strong beat. Must read at 360 px width. Labels are true ranks whatever the reveal order.
- **F5** Length (owner 2026-10-31): up to **60 s** (hard max 60 s, YouTube Shorts limit; typical 30-50 s). Length follows the footage: each entry 5-12 s, long enough to show the whole moment (setup -> action -> result), never cut short just to hit a target. No speechless stretch > 1.4 s unless a deliberate payoff beat with visible motion. (Old 26-32 s window retired.)
- **F6** Reveal order non-sequential (`ranking:reveal --n 5`), never 5-4-3-2-1 or 1-2-3-4-5, and different from the previous 3 videos. The #1 is not first and not a long static shot (batch 1 lesson: static #1 cost retention points).
- **F7** Cold open: frame 0 = real motion from the first reveal + title + rail with five empty slots + a 2-5 word caption readable sound-off at 0.0 s. Narration starts <= 0.2 s. No logo card, no "Welcome", no spoken "Top 5 ..." read-aloud of the title.
- **F8** Loop ending: last spoken line hands back to frame 0 (callback to the opening line or first reveal); no fade, no end card, <= 0.3 s tail but the last word must finish fully (restart-cut risk, fzcu v12).
- **F9** Unique end CTA per video, 15 distinct texts (same experiment logic as goal-28). On-screen text only in the last 1.5-2.5 s, caption-band style, <= 40 chars, no emoji, never spoken in the last 5 s (insights `no_outro`). Rotate families: follow-series, comment-opinion ("Your #1? Comment it"), comment-request ("Name the next category"), share, like. Record `"cta": {id, family, text}` in `script.json`; description line 1 repeats the ask. Batch-1 videos had none (control arm), so 3 baseline plus 15 test arms.
- **F12 Spoken engagement ask (owner 2026-10-31, every video).** The narrator says ONE short ask, woven into the story mid-video or under a running clip (background), never as a stop-and-pitch and never in the last 5 s. Place it between reveals, roughly at 35-65% of the runtime (e.g. after the #3 or #4 reveal, before #1). It must ride the joke and the moment on screen, <= 14 words, and use one or two of: subscribe for more · agree = like · disagree = comment · share with a friend who does this / fails like this. Good: "If your cat does this too, send it to the friend who owns one." "Disagree with this #3? Tell me in the comments." "Agree so far? Like it, and wait for number one." Bad: a generic "Like and subscribe!" or anything that breaks the countdown. Rotate wording and family across the 15 videos (no exact repeat). Record `"spoken_cta": {text, at_s, family}` in `script.json`. The on-screen end CTA (F9) stays and must not repeat the same words.
- **F10** Framing: footage box as large as the layout allows (compact title for 4:3 / low-res sources); blur-fill only around it; low-res archive is shown with an honest "archival" treatment, never claimed as upscaled.
- **F13 Full idea, no weird crops (owner 2026-10-31, channel rule; 2026-11-08: "not too zoomed so we can see what happens": whole action beats big subject; `follow.zoom` under 0.75 needs a written reason, labels and captions stay off the subject).** Every moment must show the WHOLE idea of the source clip: the setup, the action and the result, with all key subjects in frame for the whole window (both players, the ball/goal, the person and what they react to, the animal and its target). Never crop off heads, hands, feet, the ball, the landing or the punchline; never trim the window so the viewer misses why it matters. For 16:9 sources prefer `fit_box` (whole frame visible, blur-fill around it) over `fill_box`; `fill_box` only when nothing important is lost (max ~15 % per side, check every frame of the window). Punch-ins only when the subject stays fully inside the zoomed frame. If a clip cannot be shown fully and clearly in 9:16, reject that clip instead of cropping it.
- **F16 Full layout (2026-11-11, opt-in, every NEW plan).** `"title_layout": "full"` (style v2): the footage owns the whole canvas; 9:16 sources (aspect <= 0.62) are full-bleed with zero crop, other aspects contain-fit in a tall box (~y 220-1620) over the blur fill, never cropped by default (F13). The full two-line title shows 0-1.6 s over the footage (safe box, dark top scrim), then turns in 0.25 s into a one-line chip "TOP 5 W1 W2 MOMENTS" at y ~270-350; the rail (same cards) starts under the chip and appears with it; captions/hook/badge stay in the safe box. Frame 0 from `scripts/frame-pick.py` top candidates. For plans that opt in, F16 supersedes the permanent title band of F7 and Q7 (the title is on screen 0-1.6 s, the chip after it).
- **F11** Description: first line = ask/hook, then one sentence per rank in true order, then the opinion line, `Credits:` block (R6), 4-5 hashtags. No AI-generation text of any kind (owner rule 2026-10-28).

## 3. VOICE RULES (V)
- **V1 Persona.** One narrator for the whole brand: dry, quick, warm deadpan, "a nature-documentary voice that is secretly a stand-up comic", with a tiny smile. Jokes about the situation, never the person. Opinion framing ("my number one"). No clickbait ("you won't believe", "get ready for"), no "bro really said", no filler.
- **V2 TTS.** Gemini TTS voice **Puck** for all 15 (brand consistency, same as batch 1); one style prompt per video, short and concrete (what batch 1 used: "Dry, deadpan, brisk narrator with a tiny smile. Clear diction, never shouting; say every word fully to the end, especially the last word of each line."). One synthesis request per line, cache by text+voice+style (re-builds cost no quota; quota is shared with the sibling studio, so no throwaway takes). Re-roll only a line the ASR check shows mis-spoken.
- **V3 Pacing.** <= 2.8 spoken words/s over the whole video, <= 2.5 s per line, <= 8 words per line (<= 10 for a rank-intro line), total <= ~45 spoken words in 28-30 s (batch 1 ran 42-48). Builder `max_pause` 0.4 s between a rank intro and its joke. Lines sit at their slot start (`target_start_ms`) with the rank chip lit; a rank is spoken as "Number N" only when that rank is on screen.
- **V4 One joke per moment.** Exactly one fact-anchored joke line per entry (a setup/pay-off built on a specific on-screen detail at `anchor_ms`), plus at most one <= 5-word rank-intro join ("Number three: no comment."). Never restate the action; set up, pay off, undercut or call back. Angles rotate across entries (observational, reversal, contrast, deadpan, callback).
- **V5 Silent beats.** Keep the payoff silent when the picture or its sound is funnier (0.9-1.2 s, rail label lands there; bed dips -6 dB via `bed_dips`). Never talk over a key source sound (crash, splash, yelp, crowd laugh); if source audio is kept, the line moves, if source audio is unclear/unlicensed it is stripped and the picture carries the beat. Min 2 silent beats per video, the #1 payoff is one of them.
- **V6 Hook.** >= 3 hook options in the writer pass; hook works with sound off (title + caption carry it) and with sound on (adds a joke, does not read the title). Hook may name the criterion, never the #1.
- **V7 Order truth.** Transitions stay true under the real reveal order: say "number N", never "the next one" / "the highest". Callbacks to an earlier moment only if already shown (flag `order_dependent`; a reveal change forces a rewrite).
- **V8 Claims the picture cannot prove.** Only what is on screen at `anchor_ms` or on the source page (exact, attributed quote allowed). No speed, cost, record, "first ever", date or species facts that are not on the page. Labels match footage: "this robot" not "our robot" (NOAA owns the ROV); "young animals" not "baby" when a dog is not a baby; CGI is not footage; no character/director names from archival credits unless the licence needs them. Lines about something cropped out of 9:16 are removed.
- **V9 No fake quotes.** The narrator reads posture and situation; it never puts invented words in an animal's or a person's mouth.
- **V10 Safety.** Never joke about injury, disability, illness, age, race, religion, gender, nationality or other protected traits; no humiliation of private people or minors; no animals in real distress; no real danger or risky stunts framed as encouraged; no tragedy. Harmless fails, funny animals, clever skills and awkward reactions are in. Punchline-critic rejects on any breach (`advertiser_safety` 0).
- **V11 Writer/critic loop.** Writer never scores its own lines; critic never drafts. >= 3 lines per moment from different angles, <= 2 transition options per join, a loop outro. Critic scores 10 criteria /100, sees the frame at each `anchor_ms`, rejects clichés, restating, pre-explaining, unverifiable claims, mean targets, reveal breaks; lines < 70 get a note + direction with `evidence_ms`. Stop at `stabilised: true`, max 2 rewrite rounds; a third needs the orchestrator.
- **V12 Captions.** Word-accurate: build with `narration.snap_onsets: true` so caption onsets come from the line clip's own energy (batch 1 v2 had captions 0.45-0.5 s off). Run `captions_audit` and an ASR pass of the render; caption text equals spoken text (homophones such as flour/flower are noted, not "fixed"). Caption band ends y <= 1400, never collides with rail/title; one emphasis colour per phrase on the `emphasis_words`.
- **V13 Loudness / bed.** Integrated -14 +/- 1 LUFS, true peak <= -1.5 dBTP (batch 1: -14.3 / -2.0). Narration clearly above the original tone bed (`bed: "tone"`, original, no samples, no third-party score); bed dips under silent payoffs and key source sound; no audio dropouts, no DC offset, no clipping. Restoration scores on archive films are never used.
- **V14 No AI text.** Nothing in `description`, `script.json`, credits or captions says AI-generated, synthetic voice, Gemini TTS (CLAUDE.md, 2026-10-28). Source credits stay.
- **V15 Mojibake.** Every JSON written with Python uses `encoding="utf8"`; `build.ts` refuses on-screen text with mojibake. Use plain ASCII punctuation in narration and rail labels.

## 4. QA RULES (Q)
- **Q1 Gates (all must hold; code-enforced in policy `viralRankings`):** qa-director score >= 85 with no blockers; insights score >= 85 (no "must" item at 0; `qa/insights-score.json`); Retention (qa-director section) >= 80 (no rule at 0); every used source/moment countersigned by rights-scout; latest review is on the latest version. A retitle/regression build re-checks only the diff but must restate scores.
- **Q2 Automatic checks first, in this order, before any frame is opened:** `ranking:validate <plan> ` (structure, library, rights gate, title format, blocklist) -> `ranking:zones <production>` (burned-in text under rail/caption/title zones, warn = look at it) -> techqa (`qa/techqa.json` pass, black/freeze/silence/flash/dropout/loudness/true peak/duration) -> `captions_audit` + faster-whisper ASR of the render vs script -> loudness. A failed automatic check is a finding without any frame look.
- **Q3 Frame budget.** <= 12 frames, scaled <= 360 px wide, aimed at named risks (0.0/0.5/1.0/2.0 s, each rank's first strong beat, the #1 payoff, the last 3 s, one question/answer-neutral caption frame). Re-QA: only the listed defects + a regression diff, no new contact-sheet reading.
- **Q4 Video-understanding pass (mandatory, once, on the final render):** `bash scripts/video-analyze.sh <render.mp4>` (wraps the video-analyze skill; never `python3`). Use the frame+transcript package to verify (a) each line matches what is on screen at its time, (b) ranks/labels vs reveal order, (c) the claims in description match the picture. Eye-analysis errors are real (2 found in batch 1). Keep output in `qa/`, report <= 5 lines.
- **Q15 Full-idea check (F13).** For every rank, QA looks at the start, action and result frames: all key subjects fully in frame, nothing important cropped, the window shows setup -> result. Any cropped head/ball/payoff or a window that cuts the idea = major (-5); a rank you cannot understand sound-off = blocker. Length > 60 s = blocker.
- **Q5 Punch-in / crop.** Every zoom/punch-in is clipped to the footage box: it must not bleed into the title band, rail or caption band (fzcu #1 at 26-27 s). Check the biggest punch frame at 360 px.
- **Q6 Frame furniture.** No source logo, bug, watermark, sliver of a baked title card, border, black bar or letterbox remnant inside the used window (check corners of each entry's first and last frame); a credit/logo sliver is a major, a visible third-party mark a blocker.
- **Q7 Text integrity.** No mojibake ("Â·", "â€"), no clipped glyphs, title text = `script.json` title, TOP 5 and MOMENTS white and the two accents only on WORD1/WORD2, title band legible at 360 px (style v2 since 2026-11-04: always two lines "TOP 5 WORD1" / "WORD2 MOMENTS", cap height >= 24 px at 360 px; v1 one-line titles ~14 px were "small but legible"), rail sublabels legible at 360 px (batch 1 v2 failure: 8 px).
- **Q8 Rank vs reveal.** Chip/label on screen = true rank at every reveal; spoken "Number N" lands within 0.3 s of that rank's card lighting; five empty slots at frame 0; revealed ranks persist.
- **Q9 Credits.** One credit line per clip/source on screen (bottom third, readable, clear of faces/action) and in the description `Credits:` block with the exact licence-required wording (e.g. "Library of Congress ... Edison Manufacturing Co. Paper Print Collection; transfer via Wikimedia Commons"), no director/character credit where the licence forbids it. `script.json` `attribution_block` and `rights_conditions_met` filled.
- **Q10 Retention items (qa-director Retention section, rules R1-R8 adapted):** length, dead air (gaps > 1.4 s), frame-0 hook, shot change/motion every 2-3 s, emphasis on the #1 (zoom/flash/sting, not only a colour), loop seam (last frame to frame 0, tail >= 0.2 s), CTA (new end text per video on-screen + one spoken mid-video ask per F12), credit legibility. Name the drop-off-risk timestamp and one concrete fix. Common batch-1 defects: static #1 or #5 shot, tiny footage in a wide frame, captions that arrive after the joke.
- **Q11 Insights score** (`docs/insights-rubric.md`, rankings items): frames at 0.0/0.5/1.0/1.5/2.0/3.0 s, last 3 s, silencedetect on the first second, transcript of the last 5 s; write `qa/insights-score.json` and repeat `INSIGHTS nn/100` in the `review:add` summary.
- **Q12 Answers the owner would catch:** every claim in title, rail label, narration and description survives "what about X?"; "ranked by" says what it measures; no label that the footage cannot back (CGI, mission, species, era).
- **Q14 Spoken ask check (F12).** QA and retention verify, at the logged `at_s`: the spoken ask is present, falls 35-65% in, is outside the last 5 s, plays under footage (no freeze or black card), fits the moment on screen, and is different from the batch's other asks and from the end CTA. Missing ask = blocker. Generic or out-of-context wording, or an ask that stalls the countdown, = major (-5). Retention notes any audience-drop risk at that timestamp.
- **Q13 Reports.** Reviews are short: scores, <= 8 timestamped defects with fix + owner, no log dumps. QA never reviews its own build; showrunner never reviews its own render.

## 5. RIGHTS RULES (R)
- **R1 Allowed pools only:** NOAA / NASA / other US-federal-produced media (primary page checked), Library of Congress public-domain prints (LoC page, not a Commons tag), Pixabay / Pexels / Mixkit (licence on the clip page, screenshot or URL in evidence), Wikimedia Commons only when the primary uploader/transfer is verified clean, Internet Archive only with a verified original uploader, creator permission in writing (owner contacts). YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, TV broadcasts and stock sites with attribution-locked or editorial-only licences are research-only: never downloaded for a cut.
- **R2 Commons PD tag != clearance.** Overturned in batch 1: modern box-set restorations (Lumiere), scored transfers (hose gag), unknown IA uploaders, Commons-only copies of NOAA material. Prove the source and the transfer. Contractor-piloted federal footage (NOAA Dive 04, GFOE) stays "approved on NOAA publication", re-confirmed by email only if the owner sends it.
- **R3 Audio.** Picture only unless the audio itself is cleared; all narration/bed ours. Restoration or film scores, broadcast commentary, copyrighted music in the background -> strip or reject the clip.
- **R4 People.** No identifiable private individuals or minors in modern footage unless the licence/stock release covers it; no clip whose subject could be embarrassed or harmed. Pre-1930 archival people are fine when the film is PD and the line mocks the situation, not the person. **Celebrities, public figures, athletes, brand logos, trademarked characters, named products, league/club marks: not used** (stock "editorial use only" = rejected). No sports broadcast footage.
- **R5 Per-moment gate.** `rights_status` of the moment narrows the source (a visible bug or broadcast logo in one window rejects that window). Effective rights = most restrictive; the countersigner is rights-scout or owner, never the showrunner. Re-countersign when a window, crop or quote changes. Cutlist and plan refuse non-publishable moments.
- **R6 Credits.** Each source keeps the credit the licence needs on screen and in the description; credits state the producer, never "AI". No endorsement implied.
- **R7 Generated art** (Codex stills for any card/backdrop): original, no trade-dress lookalike, no third-party marks; rights-scout looks at every generated image once.
- **R8 Needs owner approval:** public name/handle changes, any spend or paid licence, contacting a creator/agency/NOAA, any footage whose licence needs a legal read (e.g. Lumiere worldwide), scheduling/publishing (browser, one video at a time, explicit OK), sports/celebrity/minor-adjacent categories, keeping or deleting research-only downloads. Caps: ~2 GB downloads per video, stop if C: < 12 GB, delete nothing.
