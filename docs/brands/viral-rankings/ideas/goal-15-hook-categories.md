## Honest caveats
- Unlikely-friend pet scores highest on demand but real mixed-species stock footage is scarce and many Pixabay animal pairs are AI-generated: run a 5-clip check first.
- Chain-reaction domino: strongest demand, thinnest licensed stock (about 3 real domino/jenga clips): medium footage risk.
- Implosion countdown: DVIDS not verified; Pixabay demolition clips are the confirmed floor.
- Ice-shelf collapse: Pixabay clips are 5 s and mostly calm; real calving action in NASA/NPS not yet confirmed.
- Sneak-wave: people-free clips only; never a person hit by a wave played for laughs.
- Hyphenated words (SPLIT-SECOND, PERFECT-TIMING, CHAIN-REACTION, SNEAK-WAVE, ICE-SHELF, UNLIKELY-FRIEND) count as one word each; none are on the hype blocklist.
