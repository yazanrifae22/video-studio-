# test-3d engine (packages/test3d/web + src/capture.ts)

Photographic table-top renderer: Three.js r187 in headless Chrome (RTX 4070 via ANGLE/D3D11), WebCodecs H.264 in the page. All assets are procedural (no downloaded HDRIs, textures, models or fonts).

## Run
- Stills: `npx tsx packages/test3d/src/dev-stills.ts <epDir> <score.json> --times 1.3,2.7 --out DIR [--sub 16] [--width 540] [--crop x,y,w,h] [--extra "tm=aces&grain=0"] [--bench]` (`--width 0 --crop ...` = native-pixel detail; page errors and warnings are printed)
- Video: `npx tsx packages/test3d/src/dev-video.ts <epDir> <score.json> --out file.mp4 [--sub 16]` (video only, 1080x1920, about 24 Mb/s; prints the SFX cues)
- Look-dev film: epDir `packages/test3d/web/lookdev`, score `packages/test3d/web/lookdev/score.json`. 16:9 stills: `dev-stills.ts ... --size 1920x1080` (checked 2026-11-15: round bokeh, square board, no stretch; 33 ms/frame at sub 16 without encode).
- Code: `open(epDir, scorePath, {width, height, sub, film, extra})` -> `{page, browser, info, close}`; `renderVideo(session, out.mp4, onProgress)`; `renderRange(session, out.mp4, f0, f1, onProgress)` (frames f0..f1-1, first frame a key frame: long-form segments and previews; page API `t3d.renderBatch(i0, n, end)`); `stills(session, times, dir, width = 540, crop?)`. `info = {frames, fps, width, height, duration, gl, sub, cues: [{name, t, db, dur?}]}`.
- Measured 2026-11-14: 40 ms/frame at sub 16 with encode (look-dev), 44 ms/frame without encode with a 3000-grain pile; boot 8 s. A 65 s film = about 1.5-2 min. Another session on the GPU can triple it.
- URL params (`extra`): `sub` (sub-frames, 16), `shutter` (0.5), `tm` (`agx` | `aces`), `bloom` (0.45), `bloomthr` (1.4), `grain` (0.02), `vig` (0.28), `fill` (1.9), `dof=0`, `contrast` (1.16), `sat` (1.12).

## Film contract
`<epDir>/film.js` default-exports `async function build(K)`: build the scene once, return `{ frame(t) }`. `frame(t)` must be a pure function of t (no timers, no state kept between calls): pose every object, place the camera, set `K.lens`, `K.exposure`. It is called 17 times per output frame with slightly different t.

## K
- `THREE, scene, camera, renderer, score, fps, duration, W, H, kit`
- `K.lens = {focus, fstop, fov, sensor}`: focus = metres from the camera to the sharp plane, measured along the view axis (use `K.focusDist(point)` after `K.look`); fstop lower = blurrier; fov = vertical degrees; sensor = sensor HEIGHT in metres, default = a full-frame 36 mm long side: 0.036 in portrait, 0.02025 at 16:9 (2026-11-15). At 16:9 the same fov + fstop therefore blurs about 0.56 x as much as in portrait (shorter focal length); open up roughly one and a half stops for the same look.
- `K.exposure` (about 0.8 wide, 1.1 macro on white rice), `K.shutter` (fraction of the frame, 0.5; 0.3 for fast camera moves).
- `K.look(pos, target, roll?)`, `K.focusDist(point)`, `K.snap(t)` (frame grid: put cuts on it), `K.v3(x, y, z)`.
- Script: `K.line(id)`, `K.word(lineId, word, n = 0)`, `K.wordEnd(...)` (n-th match, negative = from the end, case/punctuation/diacritics ignored; a missing word throws with the line's words listed).
- `K.sfx(name, t, db = -18, dur?)`, call in build(): `tick` (grain lands), `pour` (dur), `thud`, `whoosh`, `riser` (dur), `hit`, `ding`, `coin`, `tap`, `chime`, `swell` (dur). The list lives in `packages/test3d/src/audio.ts` (`SFX` table); an unknown name fails the build.
- Helpers: `K.ease.{linear,in,out,inOut,inOutQuad,smooth,smoother,outExpo,outBack}`, `K.seg(t, t0, t1, ease)`, `K.keys([[t, v], ...], ease)`, `K.spring(t, hz, damp)`, `K.lerp`, `K.clamp`, `K.smooth`, `K.rng(seed)` (`.range`, `.gauss`, `.pick`), `K.noise2`, `K.fbm`.
- `K.onFrame.push((t, camera, lens) => ...)` runs after every `frame(t)` (billboards and the like).

## Kit (`K.kit`, metres, y up, board top = y 0, board centre = origin)
- `board({dark: 'green' | 'black'})` -> Group: marble 8x8 (square `SQ` = 0.055), wood frame (0.03 wide, lip 1.6 mm), walnut table at `.tableY` (-0.029). `.sq(n)` = centre of square n (1 = a1 near-left for a camera on +z, left to right, then up). The marble mirrors whatever stands on it.
- `king()` gold, 0.096 tall, base radius 0.0196. `pawn({scale 1, tone 'maple' | 'walnut' | 'ebony' (= 'dark')})` wood, 0.050 x scale tall (scale is built into `.profile`, so tilt/topple work; a servant = `scale: 0.7`). Origin = base centre. `goldMaterial({roughness})`.
- `rice.grain({seed})` hero Mesh (own high-detail shape with furrows and a germ notch, more light through it); `.rest(point)` -> rest pose lying on that point; `.pose(pose)`.
- `rice.pile(n, centre, seed, {radius, slope, detail})` -> InstancedMesh, grains at rest (6.5 x 2.2 x 1.8 mm, +-12 %). `.rest[i] = {pos, quat, scale}` in deposit order, `.show(k)` first k grains, `.place(i, pose)`, `.reset()`, `.top` (highest point, y), `.radius`. 128 grains fit one square with `radius: 0.024`; 3000 grains build in 0.1 s and spread to r = 0.115.
- `rice.drop(target, t0, {height 0.1, slow 1, bounce 0.3, bounces 2, scatter 0.012, spin 9, settle 0.22, seed})` -> `{at(t) -> {pos, quat, scale, visible, landed}, tStart, tLand, tEnd, hits: [{t, gain}]}`. **t0 = the first touch**, the fall starts at `tStart` (real free fall: slow at the top, fastest at the touch). `slow: 6` = high-speed-camera look. `hits` are the tick times.
- `rice.flood({ground, size 1.6, res 220, grains 26000, cell 0.003, slope 0.58})` -> Group: a rice mass that GROWS. In frame(t): `.set(sources, {flow, flowLen, focus, radius, dense, lumps, edge, view})`; sources = `[{x, z, p, s?, r0?, x1?, z1?}]` (heaps with their top at height p, slope s; x1/z1 = a ridge). The surface is the highest heap at each point and shows wherever it is above `ground(x, z)`, so a tall heap runs over the frame onto the table and buries pieces by itself. `focus` + `radius` = the disc that gets REAL grains (put it where the lens looks; <= 0.27 m at one layer, `dense: 2` for macro), `flow` = a rising number while it grows (the top grains slide downhill), `view {x, z, size}` = the square the mesh covers (0.3-0.6 for close shots, the mesh cell is size / 220). `.heightAt(x, z)`, `.riceAt(x, z)`, `.detail('lo' | 'mid' | 'hi')`, `.grains.castShadow` (off; switch on for macro). Static sources cost nothing per frame; a growing mass costs about 0.1-0.3 s per frame at sub 16.
  A source may carry `rough` (0.2-0.3) + `seed`: a real poured heap (lobes and avalanche tongues, uneven slope, slumps on the face, uneven ridge crest). `flood.fallOf(source, x, z)` = how far below its top that heap is at a point (use it instead of `0.58 x distance` when a rough heap must reach an exact level somewhere). `.set` option `scatter: {reach 0.035, density 0.6}` = loose grains past the edge over the whole view (wide shots).
- `rice.pour(n)` -> InstancedMesh: rice poured in a thin stream, slow-motion look (2026-11-14, replaces `rain` in films). After `flood.set`: `.set(t, {at(ts, k) -> {x, z, r?, spread?}, top, v0, g, rate, r0, spread, sway, tOn, tOff, heightAt, bounce, kick})`: every grain has its own speed, drift and tumble, the stream widens and snakes, grains hop outward where they land, skitter and are taken up by the heap. Let it enter just above the frame (`top`), slow (`v0` 0.5-0.6, `g` 2-3) and with `K.shutter` 0.2-0.25: a fast stream seen for two frames is only streaks. Wide shots need `rate` 3000 for a column; a macro needs 250.
- `rice.rain(n)` -> InstancedMesh: the old stream (constant speed, stretched grains: reads as rain). `.set(t, {at(ts) -> {x, z, r, rz?}, top, speed, tOn, tOff, heightAt})`.
- `rice.heap(radius, height, centre, seed, {rz, grains 4000, lumps})` -> Group: dune mesh + real grains on top and around the rim. Rests on the flat plane y = centre.y (keep it on the board; it does not spill over the edge). `.heightAt(x, z)`. Keep height <= 0.75 x the short radius (angle of repose).
- `coins(n, {seed, material})` stack (27 mm x 2 mm each, `.height`; share one `coinMaterial({seed, metal: 'gold'|'silver'})` between stacks of the same metal; silver = cool white, default gold), `chips(n, {seed, like, color 'red' | 'white'})` game chips (30 x 3.3 mm, no markings; white = matte chalk, a Moon stand-in); children keep `userData.rest` (animate them landing one by one). `card()` blank dark card 85.6 x 54 x 0.9 mm with a blank chip.
- `cup({seed, material 'metal' | 'copper' | 'wood' | Material})` upturned cup (2026-11-17): 45 mm tall, mouth 18.1 mm inner radius (a 27 mm coin fits), rim bead 21.3 mm, foot ring on top; origin = rim centre on the board. Share `cupMaterial({material, seed})` between cups; brushed metal needs `stage.probe(cup, at, {intensity: 2})` (shared material = one probe for all) or it reads dark. `cups(n, {material})` -> InstancedMesh (100 cups = one draw call): `.place(i, pos, quat?, scale?)`, `.hide(i)`, `.commit()`; all start hidden; a 10 x 10 field at pitch 0.0492 puts the outer ring on the frame lip (y 0.0016).
- `liftCup(cup, home, {t0, height 0.055, tilt 1.05, dur 0.7, dir -PI/2, hold, back, index})` -> `{at(t), apply(t), tUp, tDown}`: rises (fast start, soft stop) and tips its top toward dir (default -z: the mouth turns to a camera on +z); `hold` = seconds up, then down in `back`; `index` = an instance of `cups()` (call `.commit()` after). Pure function of t.
- `paper({w 0.21, h 0.297, thick 0.0001, seed})` sheet lying on the board, centred: `.setFolds(k)` k folds in half, fraction = the top half swinging over its hinge (footprint halves, thickness doubles, longer side first then alternate; it slides back to the centre while folding), sheet lines on the edges; `.size {x, y, z}`. Readable 0..8 (A4 after 7 = 19 x 26 x 12.8 mm). `foldState(w, h, thick, k)` (table-props.js) = the same rule as numbers.
- `paperStack({w 0.03, d 0.021, pitch 0.0001, seed})` tower of sheets on y 0: `.setHeight(hm)` any height; sheet lines are a texture repeat (one per `pitch`) plus a shader ream band every 500 sheets that fades where it would alias, so 1 mm and 2 m both read as paper. Checked 2026-11-17: 5 cm beside the king, 2 m out of frame from a low camera.
- Acting: `tilt(piece, home, dir, angle)` leans a piece on its base edge (dir = radians in xz, 0 = +x); `topple(piece, home, {dir, t0, roll})` -> `{at(t), apply(t), tHit, hits}`: tips over, head hits the board at tHit, one bounce, rolls on its side along an arc and rocks to rest.
- `numberTag(text, {height 0.014, gain 1.5})` camera-facing label, `height` = digit height in metres. `.setText(s)`, `.pop` (scale), `.opacity`, `.capHeight`; options `outline: 0.09` (black outline, reads on white rice), `overlay: true` (drawn over everything: place it from the camera, see `hud()` in the quwk film), `px` (canvas font size, lower for long strings that change), `plate: 0.6` (dark rounded plate behind the text: gold digits on white rice). Never lens-blurred (it still gets motion blur and a little bloom).
- `stage({env, key, rim, fill, discs, dust, background})` -> `{key, rim, fill, discs, shadowFocus(centre, radius), probe(obj, at?)}`. Warm key upper left, rim behind right, cool fill; out-of-focus light discs behind the board (-z side, wide arc). Per frame a film may set `stage.discGain`, `stage.discSoft` (no bright edge ring), `stage.discMax` (at most n discs in the picture), and relight a shot with `stage.key.userData.dir` / `.intensity`, `stage.rim.intensity`, `stage.fill.intensity`, `scene.environmentIntensity` BEFORE `shadowFocus` (reset them at the top of `frame(t)`).
- The marble is worn (`board({wear: 1})`, `K.mirror.uniforms.uWear`): hairline scratches, dull smudges and dust break the mirror image up; `K.mirror.mesh.material.envMapIntensity` turns the room's reflections down for one shot (a lamp glinting in bare marble).

## How focus and f-stop behave
Real thin lens: aperture radius = (sensor / 2 / tan(fov / 2)) / (2 x fstop). Blur radius in pixels = (W / 2) x P00 x aperture x |1/focus - 1/depth|, so blur explodes at macro distance, as on a real camera.
- Macro (camera 5-8 cm from the subject, fov 34): f/11-f/16. Something 11 cm behind is then a soft glowing shape (about 100 px blur radius).
- Medium (15-30 cm): f/4-f/8. Wide (1 m): f/4-f/5.6 keeps the board sharp with soft near and far edges; f/2 already blurs a heap 20 cm in front.
- Rack focus = animate `K.lens.focus` between two `K.focusDist(...)` values.
- The background discs and the dust grow and fade with the blur automatically.

## Tips for a film author
- Call `stage.shadowFocus(centre, radius)` in every shot: radius 0.14 for macro, 0.3 for a row, 0.42 for the whole board. Too large = soft, weak contact shadows; anything outside the radius casts no shadow.
- Call `stage.probe(king)` once at the end of build() (after everything is placed) or the gold reflects only the room, at 36 % strength. One bake per position: keep the returned texture and switch `king.material.envMap` in frame(t) (see the look-dev film).
- Hide and show things by t inside frame(t) (`visible`, `pile.show(k)`), never with timers. Cuts on `K.snap(t)`.
- A grain needs 250+ px to read as rice: camera 5-7 cm away, low angle, fov 30-36. Expose for the rice (1.0-1.2), the gold will glow.
- Piles up to about 400 grains use the high-detail grain automatically; heaps are for wide and medium shots only.
- Fast camera moves smear tags: `K.shutter = 0.3`, and fade far tags out (they stack up in perspective).
- Gold needs something bright to reflect: keep it turned toward the key (upper left) or the rim (behind right).
- Mirror glare: the rim light sits 36 degrees right of -z, 21 degrees up. A low camera that looks within about 20 degrees of that direction gets a blown-out white patch on the marble or the table; turn the shot away from it.
- A low camera looking UP at rice sees the unlit underside: only the hero grain has enough light through it for that.
- Framing check without rendering: a film may fill `K.marks` (name -> Vector3) in frame(t) and project them with `K.camera` from a page.evaluate after `t3d.still(t, 16)` at sub 1.
- Vertical frame (QA round 1, quwk): subject in about 22-62 % of the height, bigger rather than wider; the lower frame must hold board, rice or reflections, so keep the camera over (or just outside) the marble, low and close, and aim with a "point p lands at (sx, sy)" helper (`aimAt` / `shot` in the quwk film) instead of a look-at target. A macro along the grid shows two bands of colour; a diagonal across the squares reads as a chessboard.
- sub 16 is the production setting. sub 1 = no depth of field, no anti-aliasing (layout check only). sub 32 removes the last sparkle in strongly blurred gold, at twice the time.
