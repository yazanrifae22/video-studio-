# Tiko & Pip — Series Bible (v1, 2026-10-20)

Audience: ages 2–5, co-viewing with parents. Made-for-Kids is always set. Each episode has one learning goal, one small story beat (a "wait…" moment), and its own payoff and ending. Never repost the same template.

## Characters
- **Tiko**: a round, warm-orange fox cub. Curious and brave. Tiko leads the activity ("Let's count!") and invites the viewer in. Gets at most one short spoken line per episode (`talk:true`). The narrator carries the rest.
- **Pip**: a small teal bird. Cheerful, a helper, asks questions. Pip finds or brings the surprise (the missing item, the last colour). Pip mostly reacts with jump, squash and look, and does not speak yet.
- **Narrator (Leda)**: a warm, slow preschool teacher. Talks *to* the child ("you", "friend") and praises effort.
- Moods: happy is the default. Use wow for discovery and think for "let's look". **Never use sad as a cliffhanger.** If used at all, it must resolve within the same scene.

## Look and palette
Sunny sky gradient (#6CCBFF → #E9F8FF), green hills, and a slowly drifting sun and clouds. Tiko is #FF7A45 orange and Pip is #2EC4B6 teal. Text is navy #24305E with an orange accent and the rounded Baloo font. Use big, friendly emoji props (🎃 🍎 ⭐) with one focal object type per scene. Always daytime.

## Pacing rules
- Voice tempo 0.95, maxPause 0.45, about 110–130 wpm. 60–95 words per Short (30–40 s).
- One concept per scene. Every sentence causes a visible change (pop, cast beat or scene change).
- Counting words are written as separate words ("One. Two. Three.") so each object pins to its number, about 0.8–1 s apart.
- Use `holdAfter` of 0.5–0.8 s after every payoff. Captions use 3 words at the top, and the cast stands at the bottom.
- Structure: hook (≤ 3 s, motion in the first 0.3 s) → hello and invitation → model the skill → do it together → story payoff → body or movement reinforcement → praise and goodbye.

## Do
- Praise the viewer directly ("Great job, friend!").
- Invite movement such as pointing, fingers or clapping.
- Repeat the key word 3 or more times.
- Keep both characters on screen in most scenes.
- Use a wave on hello and goodbye, and jump/cheer on the payoff.

## Don't
- Nothing scary: no ghosts, monsters, skeletons, dark or night scenes, "boo" jump-scares or creepy sounds. Use only friendly, smiling holiday props.
- No flashing (more than 3 high-contrast changes per second), no fast cuts, and no loud thuds. Avoid hook `big` figures because they trigger a −8 dB thud.
- No unsafe behaviour (climbing, knives for carving, candles or fire, eating unknown things).
- No "comment / like / bell / link in description" asks, no data collection, and no third-party characters. The only ask is the fixed, grown-up-addressed Sunny Hill subscribe line in the kit ending (see `series-bible.md` section 5; `prelint` allows only that line).
- No sad or unresolved endings, and no keyword-stuffed titles.
